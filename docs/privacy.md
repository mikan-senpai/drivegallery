# DriveGallery — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

DriveGallery is a viewer for photos and videos stored in your own Google Drive.

## What the app accesses

- **Your Google Drive photos and videos (read-only).** When you sign in, DriveGallery asks Google for permission to *see* your Drive files (`drive.readonly`). It uses this only to list, show and play your images and videos.
- **Edit access, only if you delete something.** The first time you choose **Move to Trash**, the app asks Google for permission to edit your Drive (`drive`). It uses this only to move the items you chose to your Drive Trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.
- **Your Google account email**, shown in the app so you know which account is connected.

## What stays on your device

- An index of your Drive media (names, dates, sizes, folder, thumbnail links) is stored on your phone so the gallery opens instantly.
- Thumbnails you've viewed are cached on your phone.
- Your sign-in tokens are stored in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- Your favorites, folder choices and display settings are stored on your phone.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, except requests to Google's own APIs (Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API) to load your files. DriveGallery has no server. It does not collect analytics, does not sell or share your data, and does not upload your photos anywhere.

When you share a photo, the file is downloaded to your phone and handed to the app you pick (e.g. WhatsApp). What happens after that is up to that app.

## Removing your data

- **Sign out** in the app (You → Disconnect) to revoke the app's access and delete the on-device index, cache and tokens.
- You can also revoke access anytime at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.
